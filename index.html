This Privacy Policy explains how the independent developer of Cozy Tidy: Room Cleanup (the “Game”) handles information in the Android version of the Game.

The Game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location to play.

The current release of the Game does not include in-game advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases. We do not use the Game to sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

The Game is distributed through Google Play. For further information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored game progress and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Android backup features may also handle app data, depending on device settings. You can manage local data through your Android device settings. The developer cannot remotely erase data that exists only on your device.

Security
